AI concept

A forecast says what outcome is expected and when. Its probability expresses uncertainty. Its test states what evidence will decide it. Keeping these together allows a later result to challenge the original prediction fairly.

More visitors at the watering hole

At least 27% of U.S. employer businesses will report recent AI use in the final Census reference window wholly within Q1 2027.

65% editorial probabilitySubjective judgment
Outcome window
2027-01-01 to 2027-03-31
Evidence cutoff
2027-05-31

Why this direction: The earlier readings suggest adoption is spreading. A 27% threshold asks whether that direction continues at a modest pace.

What could overturn it: Trials may stall, budgets may tighten, or businesses may find less recurring value than expected.

Baseline, sources & exact test

Starting point: 24.6% in September 21–October 4, 2026, from the October 22 release; this window crosses quarters. The final wholly within-quarter readings were 19.1% for Q1, 21.7% for Q2 and 23.8% for Q3 2026. These are snapshots, not quarter averages. [86]

The test: Use the Census Business Trends and Outlook Survey national estimate for AI use in the last two weeks, retaining the current employer-business population and question. Select the final reference window wholly inside January–March 2027. Yes at 27% or above; no below. The published point estimate decides the event; report its standard error separately. Missing data or a changed definition is unresolvable.

The forager learns to recover

At least two of OpenAI, Anthropic, Google/DeepMind, Meta and Mistral will publish qualifying new agent-reliability reports during Q1 2027.

75% editorial probabilitySubjective judgment
Outcome window
2027-01-01 to 2027-03-31
Evidence cutoff
2027-04-30

Why this direction: As agents handle longer sequences, recovering after an error and checking completed work become useful selling points.

What could overturn it: Providers may emphasize other capabilities, publish no usable comparison, or describe an aspiration rather than a new result.

Baseline, sources & exact test

Starting point: October release notes already emphasize agents and computer-based work. The existing E-02 pilot asks a related question about the remainder of 2026; it is a separate forecast with a different window. [3], [5], [7], [39], [40]

The test: Count each named provider once for a dated January–March 2027 release note or technical evaluation concerning a newly released model or agent version. Reposts and vague promises do not count. Yes with at least two qualifying providers, no with fewer after reviewing the public record. This measures published claims, not independent confirmation of performance.

How will accuracy be measured?

Hit rate is correct directional calls divided by resolved directional calls. A forecast above 50% favors yes; below 50% favors no. Exactly 50% has no directional call.

Binary Brier error measures the gap between a probability and an outcome: (probability − outcome)², with outcome 1 for yes and 0 for no. Average it across resolved eligible forecasts. It ranges from 0 (best) to 1 (worst). A 70% forecast scores 0.09 if the event happens and 0.49 if it does not. This is an illustration, not an AIEVOQ result.

Missing evidence is not a miss. Pending and unresolvable records remain visible but unscored. The original locked probability is retained, and each forecast counts once. A confidence chart will wait until enough outcomes accumulate; a few hits cannot establish well-judged confidence.
