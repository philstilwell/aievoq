Mathematics

Mathematics after the rain

AI concept

AI-assisted mathematical discovery involves proposing results, checking correctness, explaining arguments and deciding what to build on. Success at one stage does not settle the others. The disruption concerns how researchers manage all of these activities as machine-generated work enters their field.

Key statistics

Counts of proposed research, not accepted breakthroughs or independent results. Several manuscripts can belong to one family; checking stages vary.

Source: OpenAI mathematics repository, README inspected October 27, 2026. OpenAI catalogue

A sudden crop of possible results changes the habitat. Discovery is one part of the story; checking, understanding and building on it are others.

When AI proposes a result, who turns it into shared knowledge? Follow the argument through researchers, independent checks, criticism and further discoveries.

Human expertise

Who raises the next generation?

AI concept

Human expertise develops through practice, feedback and learning to recognize mistakes. AI can help people complete work, but that does not establish that they acquired the underlying judgment. The question is which kinds of assistance support learning and which bypass useful practice.

Key statistics

High-school mathematics / background study published June 2025

Assisted practice grades / GPT Base
+48%
Later unaided exam grades / GPT Base
−17%

Comparison: Both are relative to the no-AI control group, not quarter-to-quarter changes. Different tests: do not subtract them to produce a “learning loss” score.

One school, nearly 1,000 students. The teaching-oriented GPT Tutor largely avoided the exam penalty. Short-run results do not establish career-long effects.

Source: Bastani and colleagues, PNAS, June 25, 2025; abstract and Table 1. Bastani et al. paper

The nursery needs attention. If AI takes over the practice that develops expertise, how do people learn to judge the work?

The question to follow +

Does assistance help someone learn, or simply help them finish? Look for the skills people retain, the ones they stop practising, and what happens when the assistant is removed.

Expectations

The naturalist was wrong

AI concept

An AI result challenges an expectation only if we know what was expected and what the new evidence establishes. Dated predictions, unstated assumptions and claims of surprise need different treatment. Comparing them carefully shows which beliefs actually need revision.

Yesterday’s field guide needs an appendix. Revisit predicted limits, surprising abilities and the assumptions hiding between them.

The question to follow +

What was actually predicted, and what would count as a surprise? Keep earlier expectations dated, then seal new forecasts in envelopes with clear conditions for being wrong.

Demand and its consequences

A crowded watering hole

AI concept

Demand for AI has several signs: current use, intended future use, willingness to pay and investment in capacity. These measures describe different decisions. Business surveys help establish how widely AI is used without proving that every user pays or that every use is productive.

Key statistics

U.S. employer businesses / October 22 release

Report recent AI use
24.6%
Expect use within six months
28.6%

Comparison: Recent use: Q1 19.1% → Q2 21.7% → Q3 23.8% (+2.1 percentage points from Q2). Latest: +0.8 points from Q3. Expected use: Q2 24.5% → Q3 27.7% → latest 28.6%.

Quarter figures are selected reference windows, not averages. The latest window, September 21–October 4, spans two quarters. History uses the October workbook; expectations are not outcomes.

Source: U.S. Census Bureau, Business Trends and Outlook Survey; October 22, 2026 release, October 27 workbook. Census workbook

AI has an appetite, and so do its customers. Follow the difference between curiosity, a useful habit, a paying customer and a supplier’s bet on the future.

Growing demand changes the habitat: electricity, computing capacity, human attention, and who gets a place at the water.

01 / Abilities

New tricks & unexpected turns

AI concept

An AI capability gain is a newly demonstrated ability or a measured improvement over an earlier baseline. To identify a gain, specify the task, conditions and role of any added tools. A striking example alone does not establish a dependable new capability.

Key statistics

15.7%

Comparison: +56.7 percentage points between model versions. This is a comparison published together in Q4, not a measured Q3-to-Q4 trend.

Anthropic’s evaluation; no independent rerun or uncertainty estimate in this record. Test success does not establish everyday workplace reliability.

02 / Speciation

The speciation hearing

AI concept

AI specialization means developing a distinct, persistent role or set of capabilities. A new product name does not establish a new technical lineage or functional type. The proposed 'speciation' test asks whether differences in function, history and dependence justify a lasting category.

A new creature, or a new coat? Look for lasting differences in role, ancestry and dependence before declaring a new branch.

October field note: the Elephant-Goldfish workflow separates design from fresh review. A new division of labor does not automatically establish a new species.

03 / Adaptability

A change in the weather

AI concept

AI adaptability is the ability to remain useful when conditions change, such as higher costs, unfamiliar inputs or loss of a tool. It must be assessed for a specified task and setting. The amount of human rescue needed is part of that assessment.

Raise the cost, remove a tool, change the rules. Which creatures still finish the job, and how much help do they need?

04 / Trade-offs

The price of feathers

AI concept

The value of an AI improvement depends on what it delivers and what it requires: money, computation, time and human checking. Comparing these trade-offs helps establish whether a stronger result is also a useful improvement for a particular job.

Key statistics

Comparison: First saved price baseline; no like-for-like earlier-quarter capture. These are charges for equal billed quantities, not equal-quality results.

One million input plus 250,000 output tokens (pieces of billed text); prompts at most 100,000 tokens. Excludes discounts, tools, retries, taxes and human checking.

Source: Anthropic model documentation and OpenAI release notes; rates checked October 28, 2026. Basket charges calculated by AIEVOQ. Anthropic rates · OpenAI rates

An impressive adaptation can be expensive to maintain. Count money, time and human attention alongside the new capability.

The lens and the evidence

Keep the metaphor curious.
Keep the evidence close.

Variation, inheritance, selection, specialization, cooperation and changing environments give us a vocabulary for following AI. The comparison becomes useful when it reveals a question worth investigating.

The creatures are invented; the reporting follows real developments. Larger ideas draw on multiple sources, including academic research. Clear explanations come first, with a source trail for readers who want to go further.

Where does the analogy bend? +

People deliberately design AI and shape the conditions around it. Models, working systems, products and companies have different histories. A biological metaphor helps us notice shared dynamics; it does not establish literal species or make progress inevitable.
