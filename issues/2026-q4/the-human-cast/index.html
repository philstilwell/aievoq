The human cast

By Phil Stilwell · Reviewed October 30, 2026 · Q4 in progress

AI concept

AI organizations decide which capabilities to release, who may use them and what restrictions apply. Those choices shape both access and deployment. The portraits introduce public figures associated with these decisions; their props are explanatory inventions, not evidence of private motives.

Keepers and habitat engineers

Every artificial jungle has people moving the fences, tending the seedlings and deciding which gates to open. These caricatures give that human cast a face and a memorable prop. The roles are real; the miniature scenes are playful inventions.

A recurring question: when a new ability leaves the greenhouse, what changes for the people outside it? The portraits are mnemonic characters, not assessments of anyone's private motives.
