Paths into the wider world

By Phil Stilwell · Reviewed October 30, 2026 · Q4 in progress

AI concept

AI can reach users through existing platforms or act through physical machines such as robots. Distribution changes who can use a capability; physical action adds demands involving perception, control and safety. The two routes create different opportunities and dependencies.

Mark Zuckerberg and Elon Musk

An AI capability can spread through familiar platforms or reach outward into physical machines. Those journeys bring different opportunities and dependencies. Here, bridges and a trailing cord give us something concrete to remember when the story turns to distribution, connections and the world beyond a chat window.

An opening cast, not a complete census. Future portraits can introduce other regions, researchers and institutions as they enter the story. The eight people here do not stand in for the many teams doing the work.
