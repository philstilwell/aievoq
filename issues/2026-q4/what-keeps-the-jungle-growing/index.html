The human cast / 1 min read

What keeps the jungle growing

By Phil Stilwell · Reviewed October 30, 2026 · Q4 in progress

AI concept

AI systems need computing hardware and organizations able to supply and operate them. Equipment costs and competition among suppliers influence which systems can reach users. These portraits connect technical capabilities with the resources and commercial conditions that support them.

Jensen Huang and Arthur Mensch

New abilities need somewhere to live and resources to run on. A species can look magnificent in its portrait and still struggle with the feeding bill. These pictures bring computing resources and competitive conditions into view, without asking the reader to memorize a hardware catalogue.

Follow the growing conditions: changes in computing supply, cost and access can alter the fortunes of many creatures at once. A new habitat can favor a different kind of specialist.
