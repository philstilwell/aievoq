The human cast / 1 min read

From puzzles to worlds

By Phil Stilwell · Reviewed October 28, 2026 · Q4 in progress

AI concept

AI methods developed for one problem can be adapted to others, including scientific research. Spatial AI addresses a related challenge: representing places, objects and their relationships so systems can reason about a world beyond a flat image. These portraits introduce those two directions.

Demis Hassabis and Fei-Fei Li

Some of the most interesting AI stories begin when a method travels. A system developed for one kind of problem becomes useful somewhere else. These two portraits offer visual routes into that idea: from games to scientific questions, and from a picture to the space it depicts.

Watch for a migration: what changes when a capability moves into a new setting, and what still has to be learned there? The miniature objects illustrate that question; they are not performance measurements.
