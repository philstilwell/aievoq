What keeps the creatures coming?

By Phil Stilwell · Reviewed October 30, 2026 · Q4 in progress

AI concept

Interest in AI becomes sustained demand when people repeatedly find it useful. Repeated use, paid renewal and a supplier's investment are different signals. Keeping them separate helps distinguish a lasting need from experimentation or a bet on future growth.

Four signs of demand, four different stories

Eight arms make an impression. Customers care whether the Relay Octopus finishes the job, and how much help it needs.

Research offers reasons for continued demand. One workplace study finds gains from AI assistance in customer support. Survey research finds workers using it across many kinds of tasks. Useful assistance can create reasons to return, even before an AI can perform an entire job. These findings come from different settings; neither guarantees a benefit in every workplace. [46], [72]

Sign of demandWhat it reveals and leaves open
Interest and trialsPeople are curious or willing to experiment. A trial can end without a useful habit.
Repeated useAI has found a recurring role. Free or bundled use may still reveal little about willingness to pay.
Paid renewals and spendingCustomers continue buying at the offered price. Provider profit still depends on the cost of serving them.
New computing capacitySuppliers are preparing for demand. Building capacity is a bet on future customers, not proof they have arrived.

Adoption has an apprenticeship. Research on major technologies explains why benefits can require complementary investment in skills and organization. The AI surveys likewise show that the depth of use matters. Buying an assistant and learning how to work with it are separate achievements. [71]-[73]

Lower costs, easier access and more reliable results could bring additional tasks within reach. The counter-pressure is equally ordinary: checking mistakes, reorganizing work and training people can consume the apparent saving. The cost of the whole meal includes the time spent picking out the bones. [40], [56]
