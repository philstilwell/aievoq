Expectations & forecasts / 1 min read

Predictions in sealed envelopes

By Phil Stilwell · Reviewed October 30, 2026 · Q4 in progress

AI concept

A testable AI forecast states an outcome, a deadline and the evidence that would count for or against it. Preserving those conditions lets later results challenge the prediction fairly. A vague claim of eventual progress cannot be assessed in the same way.

Give tomorrow a fair chance to embarrass us

A forecast can gain or lose support before its deadline. Judging it fairly means returning to the outcome it actually predicted. This quarter may also reveal that an expectation was never precise enough to test.

The transcript predicts fully autonomous AI research within one or two years. Its publication date and original speakers are not yet established, so we cannot responsibly start that countdown. Nor does AI helping with research settle how quickly any feedback loop will run. Time, resources, experimental checks and human decisions still belong in the account.
