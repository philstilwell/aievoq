Work under pressure

By Phil Stilwell · Reviewed October 30, 2026 · Q4 in progress

AI concept

AI exposure describes how much of an occupation's work could potentially be affected by AI. Comparing employment across exposure groups can reveal pressure worth investigating. It cannot, by itself, show that AI caused a job loss or that an employer adopted the technology.

Two clocks matter. September describes employment compared with September a year earlier. Its release arrived on October 21. It belongs in the Q4 news chronology and the Q3 observation record. An October publication is not automatically an October outcome.

Keep the original readings. These are the figures in each monthly announcement, not a single revised data extract. Changes in the sample or revisions may contribute to movement. Confidence intervals are not supplied by these releases. [79], [87]-[89]

Quarterly rule: compare quarter-end months when available; show the latest month in an unfinished quarter. Never add monthly annual-growth rates to create a count of lost jobs. Exposure tracks potentially affected tasks, not whether a particular employer adopted AI. The laborans case files separately follow actual dismissals, hiring, pay and new opportunities.
