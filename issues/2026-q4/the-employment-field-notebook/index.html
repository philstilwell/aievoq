The employment field notebook

By Phil Stilwell · Reviewed October 30, 2026 · Q4 in progress

AI concept

A job contains multiple tasks. Automating some tasks can change the work without eliminating the whole job, while changes in demand can affect hiring, pay and new roles. These outcomes require separate measures rather than a single total of jobs supposedly lost to AI.

In Connell's classic barnacle experiments, competition restricted where a species could live. That supplies an image for a narrowing niche. Labor economics supplies a separate explanation: automation can displace human tasks while new tasks create demand for workers. Neither guarantees that displaced people receive the new opportunities. [76], [77]

Track to followWhat the record must distinguish
Lost groundActual dismissals or reduced hours; the affected work, event date and evidence for AI's contribution.
Empty nesting sitesFewer entry roles or hires, separate from layoffs. What becomes of the route into expertise?
Changed feeding groundsTasks and pay changing within jobs that still exist; record who gains and who loses.
Working partnershipsA documented benefit from AI use, including quality, full human effort and the worker's share of the gain.
New niches and migrationNew work and movement into it; skills, location, transition time and access for displaced workers.

Q4 case files / first evidence

Pressure on the nursery: an October 21 payroll release records weaker employment in highly exposed occupations, especially among young adults. It does not identify the cause of every departure. [79], [80]

A habitat redesigned: Barclays announces expected workforce reductions on October 22; the union disputes the approach. Follow implementation and worker outcomes next. [82], [83]

An advantage borrowed: October research publications examine assisted task performance and what people understand without the tool. Career benefits remain a separate question. [84], [85]

Evidence rule: record event and publication dates separately; compare with the wider sector and check alternative explanations. AI capability, employer attribution and demonstrated job loss are different evidence. General conclusions need independent sources, with academic research explaining the mechanisms.

Reporting status / October 28: the following dated pages begin the record. A verified total of Q4 AI-caused job losses, sustained worker benefits and routes into replacement jobs remain open. Keep these outcomes separate; no survival score is assigned.
