Humans, learning & work / 2 min read

A partnership worth keeping

By Phil Stilwell · Reviewed October 28, 2026 · Q4 in progress

AI concept

Human-AI complementarity means that people and AI contribute different useful abilities to a shared task. Better combined output does not tell us who receives the gains or bears the costs. A worthwhile partnership also needs attention to pay, control, learning and dependence.

What does the human actually gain?

Gains varied across workers. This is a work-output measure, not a 15% wage gain, headcount reduction or economy-wide productivity estimate.

Source: Brynjolfsson, Li and Raymond, Generative AI at Work, Quarterly Journal of Economics; published February 4, 2025. [46]

A good neighbour can make a larger harvest possible. The next question is who gets the harvest, and what the arrangement costs.

A workplace study found that AI assistance improved customer-support performance, with differing gains across workers. A separate research synthesis found that combining people and AI did not reliably outperform the better of either alone. Useful assistance and a superior partnership are different achievements. [46], [78]

Partnership checkWhat would count as an advantage?
Purpose and controlThe person can choose useful goals, change course and challenge an unsuitable result.
Quality and effortBetter usable work, or less human effort for comparable quality, including checking and corrections.
Who receives the gains?More income, time, security or worthwhile responsibility for the worker; record employer and supplier gains separately.
Learning and judgmentThe person can explain important decisions and assess unfamiliar results. More output alone does not establish learning.
Room to moveA workable alternative if access, price or conditions change; record the costs of switching and recovery.

Symbiosis, with the terms visible. The person supplies goals, context and checks; the system supplies useful capabilities. This is a comparison of functions, not a claim that AI has biological needs or interests. Correcting an answer does not automatically retrain the underlying model.

The checking burden matters. Research on overreliance suggests that encouraging deliberate thought can improve decisions, with trade-offs in effort and experience. A human signature is not sufficient evidence of supervision. [57]

Status: these are proposed reporting questions, not a validated score or a guarantee of employment. Follow a particular person, task and workplace over time; record benefits, dependency and setbacks together.
