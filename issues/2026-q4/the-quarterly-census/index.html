The quarterly census

By Phil Stilwell · Reviewed October 30, 2026 · Q4 in progress

AI concept

AI progress has several measurable dimensions: use, effects on work, task capability, operating prices and resource demand. Each requires its own definition, population and date. Tracking them separately makes it possible to test broad claims about how AI is changing.

The creatures supply a memorable setting. The numbers tell us what is changing: how widely AI is used, where work is under pressure, what systems can accomplish, what they cost and what resources they consume.

A recurring record, with different clocks. Survey and employment releases arrive frequently. Capability results appear when testing finishes. Price comparisons need dated captures. Energy estimates arrive more slowly. Each quarter can show the latest reading without pretending that every reading measures that quarter.

The comparison is part of the number. A percentage needs a population. A score needs a task and a testing budget. A price needs a specified quantity. An impressive change deserves a look at whether the measuring method changed too.
