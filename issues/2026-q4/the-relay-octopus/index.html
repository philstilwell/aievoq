The Relay Octopus

By Phil Stilwell · Reviewed October 30, 2026 · Q4 in progress

AI concept

An AI agent uses a model to choose actions and software tools to carry them out. Its abilities depend on the tools, information and permissions it receives. The Relay Octopus represents these added capabilities and the outside connections they require.

Useful arms; borrowed equipment

The Relay Octopus occupies a workplace full of drawers, documents, and locked doors. Its stylus and paper tray represent tools. Its key represents permission. An empty cuff makes an important distinction visible: some of its abilities are attached by people and can be removed again.

This issue's example: the Gemini workplace system. [5]-[6] The portrait represents the combination of model, tools, and access. The arms are symbols of reach, not a measured claim about how many jobs it can perform at once.

Field question: when a connection fails, can it find another authorized route or explain what it needs?

Keep recognizable: teal mantle, copper freckles, detachable implements, and the trailing cord. The cord is part of the character's story: reach can bring dependency.
