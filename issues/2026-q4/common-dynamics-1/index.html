The evolutionary lens / 2 min read

Common dynamics / 1

By Phil Stilwell · Reviewed October 28, 2026 · Q4 in progress

AI concept

AI systems differ in their capabilities and costs. Developers reuse successful components, while users and institutions influence which designs spread. These are distinct processes: producing a variation, carrying something forward and favoring one option over another.

What changes, what is inherited, what spreads

These are comparisons of processes. The papers establish particular mechanisms and examples; the cross-domain map is our synthesis.

Shared dynamicIn living systemsIn AI development
1. VariationIndividuals differ; new genetic combinations and mutations supply further differences.Changed training, learned settings and system designs create alternatives. Some methods explicitly generate populations of variants. [60], [62], [63]
2. InheritanceDescendants retain features of earlier generations, allowing changes to accumulate.Earlier model settings can be retained; teaching from another model offers a different transmission route. [60], [64], [65]
3. SelectionHeritable differences can affect representation in later generations.Training procedures can preferentially copy better performers. Across products, selective retention needs evidence of reuse and continued development. [60], [62], [63]
4. Local adaptationAn advantage depends on the habitat; different conditions can favor different specialists.Task-specific training and evaluation make usefulness conditional on the job and setting. There need not be one universal winner. [60], [64], [40]
5. Constraints and trade-offsUseful functions can be costly to maintain; dependence can sometimes reduce that burden.Accuracy, speed, computing cost and human checking can pull in different directions. Better designs can also improve several together. [54], [55], [36], [40]
6. Recombination and transferInherited material can be reshuffled; evolution can work with existing components.Network designs, model-derived knowledge and tools can be combined or transferred. Combining working parts does not guarantee a working whole. [62], [65], [66]

The reporting test: identify the variant, the thing carried forward, and the reason it was retained. A rising score alone does not establish all three.
