An empty perch and a new field station

By Phil Stilwell · Reviewed October 30, 2026 · Q4 in progress

AI concept

An AI model's availability and the understanding of its output are separate developments. A provider can remove a model from a product without withdrawing it everywhere. Researchers can also improve an explanation of an AI-produced result without changing the model that produced it.

October 14 / Local disappearance

OpenAI recorded GPT-5.5's retirement from ChatGPT, Work, and Codex while leaving access through its software interface unaffected. [7] In the field notebook, the correct label is a disappearance from particular habitats. The record does not establish extinction everywhere.

This distinction will matter whenever we count survivors. An old creature can lose a prominent perch without losing all useful work. A model, a product, and the company behind them have separate lives.

October 15 / Funding the work of understanding

The Simons Foundation announced a partnership with Anthropic and OpenAI committing $15 million in combined funding and resources. It described support for evaluating AI-assisted results, learning tools, exchanging views, and developing standards, with the foundation managing the funding. [15]

This is a visible response to a changing habitat. The arrival of mathematical output has created work around it: reading groups, explanations, checks, and ways for a community to decide what should count as progress.

October 16 / A human team finds a cleaner web

A preprint by Ilia Binder, Oleg Ivrii, Istvan Prause, and Stanislav Smirnov reports a shorter proof of a stronger statement after examining AI-produced work around Brennan's conjecture, a long-standing mathematical problem. The authors describe the interaction positively. This is a specific human-authored research report; it does not certify the entire AI collection. [16]

The episode gives us a more interesting story than a simple replacement contest. An AI-produced argument can become material for human reconstruction, simplification, and extension. Whether that pattern spreads is an open question worth following.

Field note / symbiosis: different participants can contribute different strengths to a shared result. The successful unit may be the partnership, and its dependencies should remain visible.
