A nimble forager; a community pushes back

By Phil Stilwell · Reviewed October 30, 2026 · Q4 in progress

AI concept

Computer-use AI operates software through actions such as reading a screen, clicking controls and entering information. A test score measures success under specified conditions. Improvements can make routine automation more useful, but they do not automatically establish reliability across everyday workplaces.

Source: Anthropic, Introducing Claude Haiku 5.5, October 7, 2026. [3]

Anthropic introduced Haiku 5.5 for frequent, time-sensitive work. Its reported computer-use results suggest a considerable change within the Haiku family. [3]

The Ledgerfinch's ecological advantage would come from doing useful work repeatedly at a tolerable cost. The chart gives us a reason to investigate. It does not show how the bird will cope with every unfamiliar website or a working day full of interruptions.

A convincing field encounter would give competing systems the same job and count the cost of help, retries, and corrections. The winner in that habitat would be the one that reliably earns its keep.

Back in the mathematical clearing

The day after OpenAI's release, the Association for Human Mathematics objected to the publication model and urged mathematicians to stop collaborating with OpenAI. Its statement placed human understanding at the center of mathematical progress. This was the position of an organized group, not a vote of the whole profession. [14]

The dispute reaches beyond whether a particular answer is correct. It concerns what the community is for, who sets its agenda, and whether a flood of results strengthens or disrupts the human activity that makes those results meaningful.

Our evolutionary analogy needs room for both the incoming organism and the inhabitants whose habitat is being rearranged.
