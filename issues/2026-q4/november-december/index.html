By Phil Stilwell · Reviewed October 30, 2026 · Q4 in progress

AI concept

An AI announcement opens questions that take time to answer: whether a result is verified, a capability remains reliable and people find it useful. Following those outcomes through the rest of the quarter separates initial claims from what persists in practice.

The unwalked part of the trail

These months lie beyond the October 30 cutoff. The following are questions for the next visits, not completed events.

Revisit the mathematical clearing. Which proposed results have received convincing independent explanations? Which have been corrected or withdrawn? Are new methods traveling between research groups? Are the tools available to people outside the companies that built them?

Revisit the nursery. Are people developing independent judgment, or merely producing more with assistance? Look for practice, feedback and tested fallbacks; keep employment claims separate from evidence of learning.

Return to the economical foragers. Do their low prices translate into lower costs for useful work? Has a specialist kept its niche, or has a rival made its advantage ordinary?

Return to the design table. Look for independent comparisons of fresh-session review, with confirmed defects, total cost and human checking time. The Elephant-Goldfish announcement is a starting point, not an outcome series. [93], [94]

Follow the cords. As workplace assistants connect to more tools, watch both the new abilities and the new points of failure. The Relay Octopus should occasionally be drawn with something unplugged.

Hold another speciation hearing. Keep the candidate list short. Seek persistent differences rather than the excitement of a new name.

Take the closing census. Record arrivals, changed roles, and disappearances across the same selected habitats. Include failures and abandoned candidates so the history does not become a parade of winners.

Reopen the expectation ledger

At year-end, revisit envelopes E-01 to E-03: independent mathematical follow-up, reported improvements in sustained agent work, and the public evidence for autonomous model development. Record both the outcome and any ambiguity. Preserve the October 27 wording, including predictions that turn out to be unhelpful.

The broader survival questions remain open. An economical specialist may gain territory, but low unit prices alone will not tell us whether it earns its keep.
