The three sealed envelopes

By Phil Stilwell · Reviewed October 30, 2026 · Q4 in progress

AI concept

These forecasts concern mathematical follow-up, sustained agent work and AI's role in developing successor models. Each needs a distinct observable outcome. Public claims, independently established results and work completed without human intervention are different standards of evidence.

Original wording / recorded October 27, 2026

EnvelopeWhat this draft expectsWhat would count against it
E-01 / The symbiotic webAt least two additional public technical follow-ups, from distinct teams outside OpenAI, explicitly build on or correct the October mathematics collection after October 27.Fewer than two qualifying follow-ups by year-end. A news article or repeated company claim does not qualify.
E-02 / The patient foragerAt least two AI providers publish new agent release notes or evaluations reporting improved recovery from errors, preservation of task state, or checking of completed work.Fewer than two providers by year-end. Ordinary product launches without those details do not qualify.
E-03 / The self-breeding machineThe year closes without an independently assessed public demonstration of two successive AI-model development cycles completed without substantive human intervention.Such a demonstration appears, documents human help and resources, and shows improvement on tests kept separate from development.

E-01 measures public follow-up, not accepted mathematical truth. E-02 measures what providers report, not proof that every claimed improvement works. E-03 concerns the public record; it cannot reveal what happened privately. Human intervention includes designing the experiments or selecting the successor, beyond establishing the initial goal and resources.

The book's recurring pleasure: reopen the envelopes. Print the prediction beside the outcome, retain an awkward miss, and explain what changed our minds. If the criteria themselves need repair, show the old wording before replacing it. Even the naturalist must adapt.
