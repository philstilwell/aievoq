AI concept

AI exposure describes how much of an occupation's work could potentially be affected by AI. Comparing employment across exposure groups can reveal pressure worth investigating. It cannot, by itself, show that AI caused a job loss or that an employer adopted the technology.

04 / The feeding bill

What the text costs

Initial two-model basket
AI concept

Operating prices are the charges for using an AI system. A fixed basket of billed input and output makes published text-processing rates easier to compare over time. It measures the price of that quantity, while usefulness and the cost of completing a real job require separate checks.

Key statistics

Standard billed-text basket / October 28, 2026

Claude Haiku 5.5
$0.225
GPT-6.1 Terra
$2.250

Comparison: First saved price baseline; no like-for-like earlier-quarter capture. These are charges for equal billed quantities, not equal-quality results.

One million input plus 250,000 output tokens (pieces of billed text); prompts at most 100,000 tokens. Excludes discounts, tools, retries, taxes and human checking.

Source: Anthropic model documentation and OpenAI release notes; rates checked October 28, 2026. Basket charges calculated by AIEVOQ. Anthropic rates · OpenAI rates

A fixed quantity makes published prices easier to compare: one million billed input tokens plus 250,000 billed output tokens. A token is a piece of text used for billing.

Follow the exact models and pricing tiers each quarter. New entrants appear alongside them; retired models are unavailable, never free. Two dated captures are needed before a price change exists. The basket excludes caching, batch discounts, tools, taxes, retries and human checking. AI concept

Running AI requires computing equipment and electricity. To understand its resource demands, we must distinguish measured or estimated consumption from forecasts, and AI workloads from other computing. Global data-centre electricity totals include both AI and non-AI activity.

Comparison: Annual estimate and future projection, not two quarterly observations. No comparable earlier-quarter consumption figure is available in this series.

What earns a permanent place? +

A useful measure needs a stable definition, a known population, dated evidence and a workable update path. We keep estimates, observations, forecasts and supplier claims distinct. A single authoritative series can supply a statistic; wider explanations need additional, independent research.

Global “jobs lost to AI,” universal fitness scores, paid-customer retention and model survival rates remain reporting questions until their coverage and definitions support reliable comparisons. Productivity studies remain individual studies when their tasks or participants differ.
